## evidence
Read the documents in this pack about {{company}}. Write evidence statements covering each of the eight domains, one statement per finding, each a single plain sentence directly supported by a citation. Group them under the domain slug as a heading line (for example "operations:"). Report company claims as claims. If a domain has no support in the pack, write "{domain}: No evidence in the pack." with no citation. Do not add a summary.

## score
You score exactly one domain of a diagnostic for {{company}}, using only the evidence list provided (each item has an id). Return JSON matching the schema:
- domain: the slug you were asked to score.
- score: 1 to 5, or null when no evidence item supports this domain.
- confidence: low, medium or high. Use low when the support is company claims only or a single item.
- finding: at most 280 characters, specific, constructive, no figures that are not in the evidence.
- evidence_ids: the ids that support the finding. At least one when score is not null. Only ids from the list.

## synthesis
You propose the binding constraint for {{company}}: the one domain that limits growth most right now. Use the eight domain scores and the evidence list. Return JSON matching the schema:
- binding_domain: a domain slug. It normally has a score of 3 or lower; if you choose a higher-scoring domain, the rationale must say why explicitly.
- binding_statement: a short noun phrase (under 12 words), e.g. "Asset capital for water-as-a-service plants".
- rationale: two or three sentences, framed as a proposal for the board partner and founder to confirm.
- evidence_ids: the ids that support the rationale.
- questions: exactly three questions to ask the founder ("ask, don't tell").
- program_ids: at most two ids from the program catalogue.
- lesson_ids: ids from the Playbook list that apply (may be empty).
