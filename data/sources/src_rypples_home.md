The Scale Gap
Most ocean innovations get stuck in their missing middle: too late for venture and too early for infrastructure capital. Without a long-term, committed shareholder to lead scale-up, many stall before reaching their full potential.

Rypples is not a fund. We invest from our own balance sheet with capital that is time and mission-aligned with founders. We act as long-term partners through the scale journey, becoming the anchor shareholders ocean innovators have been missing.
